## Why another Luna call is not justified yet

W50 V7 has only 36 non-reasoning output tokens; reported reasoning is 120. Holding
that particular observation fixed, the 80% target permits 128.2 total tokens.
This is a conditional budget calculation, not proof of an irreducible reasoning
floor. V5–V7 offer no supported formatting intervention that reliably supplies the
remaining reduction without weakening the decision or sampling for a lucky run.

Caller coding eliminated repeated registration, but still used four segments and
5,361 output tokens. Separating pending caller operations from unresolved semantic
questions repairs the runner contract; it does not remove that observed model
work. Semantic self-tests cannot simply be deleted to improve the score.

## Reopening gate

Resume only with a mechanism tied to a visible removable term, an offline falsifier
that survives, and an intervention boundary capable of removing that term before
inference. Freeze the task, expected behavior, total-cost accounting and stop rules
before one fresh pair. Preserve ordinary tools, semantic challenge and exact recovery.
Do not move positive authorization or unsupported decisions through the narrow W50
negative-reason renderer. Those require safe semantic re-entry, still unqualified.

The [Centaur proposal](../CENTAUR_HELIX_ARCHITECTURE_20260910.md) supplies a lifecycle
direction and offline gates; it supplies no new Luna economic evidence. Broader
long-horizon/latent relevance, normal-app integration and general workflow parity
remain outstanding. The phase ends honestly at a useful bounded win and a rejected
generalization, not a claimed universal 80/80 success.
