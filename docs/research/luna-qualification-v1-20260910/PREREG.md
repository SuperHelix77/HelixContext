# Luna normal-final qualification v1

This package is a fresh, bounded Luna High qualification lane. It does not
modify historical candidates or production configuration.

## Frozen candidate

The candidate is the frozen `luna-coding-v1-75` profile plus one soft internal
working clause: concise English and familiar code/math notation when exact
meaning is preserved; retain analysis, uncertainty, checks and tools; expand
when useful; keep the final answer ordinary and model-written. This is a
steering hypothesis, not hidden-reasoning inspection or a token budget.

The candidate and control both retain High effort, ordinary tools, semantic
review and the same task sources. Candidate caller preparation (memory receipt,
profile attachment and task-local skill registration) is an explicit separately
accounted switch. No final renderer, selector JSON, translation pass, output
cap, lower effort or suppressed Engine route is allowed.

## Fixed cells and stopping

The seven equal-weight cells are intervals, dependencies, transactions,
maintenance, selection, cold recovery and W50. Existing fixtures and graders
are reused where applicable; this is exposed development material, not a
hidden holdout. Fresh Luna High candidate/control pairs are run in frozen order.
Every attempted native stream, retry, failed check, preparation, recovery and
caller I/O is retained. There are no blind retries. A binding, runtime or
configuration failure stops the lane; a behavioral failure remains a result.

Coding, maintenance, selection and cold answers must be complete ordinary final
messages. W50 must acknowledge each event and author the complete final answer
after the delayed-relevance request. The final answer is never caller-rendered.

## Accounting and limits

Record total, cached and uncached input, output, reported reasoning, other
output, native streams/turns, tool calls, wall time, local preparation and
recovery/I/O. Report paired percentages per cell, medians only for complete
compatible strata, and ratio-of-totals separately. Currency/quota savings are
not inferred from token counters. A 75/75 fallback and the aspirational 80/80
target are both visible; neither is awarded from an incomplete or incompatible
cohort. Finite check passes do not establish universal intelligence parity.
